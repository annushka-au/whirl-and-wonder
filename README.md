# Whirl & Wonder: Interactive Art & Cards

*A little magic, one spin at a time.*

Welcome to **Whirl & Wonder**, a growing collection of interactive digital art and greeting cards where scattered mosaic fragments gradually come together to reveal beautiful surprises.

Each experience combines creative coding, animation, and the simple magic of movement.

## Explore the Collection

### Whirl & Wonder I — Interactive Cards

[Play Interactive Cards](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/cards/)

Our original collection features five images:

- A sparkling rose
- An adorable puppy
- A blue-eyed husky
- A dramatic stormy sea
- A brown bear cub surrounded by nature

Move your mouse or finger in circles to assemble each picture from scattered fragments.

Add your own personal greeting and save the completed image.

### Whirl & Wonder II — Two Directions, Two Secrets

[Play Two Directions](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/two-directions/)

Discover two different worlds hidden within one interactive mosaic.

- **Rotate counterclockwise (↶)** to reveal a magnificent snow leopard surrounded by snowy mountains.
- **Rotate clockwise (↷)** to reveal a mysterious black panther in a lush tropical jungle.
- **Reverse direction** to scatter the current image and gradually assemble the other.

Hundreds of animated fragments respond to your movements, creating a magical transition between winter and the tropics.

*Two worlds. One turn.*

## How to Play

1. Open an experience in your browser.
2. Move your mouse or finger in circles over the image.
3. Watch the scattered fragments gradually assemble.
4. In *Two Directions*, reverse your rotation to discover the other animal.
5. Use **Reveal now** to complete the picture instantly.
6. Save your completed artwork as a PNG image.

In *Interactive Cards*, you can also add your own greeting or personal wish.

## Technology

Built with:

- HTML5 Canvas
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Interactive animations
- Mouse and touch controls

No frameworks, external libraries, or installation required.

The games run directly in modern desktop and mobile browsers.

## About the Project

Whirl & Wonder is an independent creative coding project exploring the relationship between movement, interaction, and digital art.

The project experiments with how simple gestures can transform scattered visual fragments into meaningful images.

Each experience introduces a different interaction, turning the process of revealing an image into part of the artwork itself.
