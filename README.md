# Whirl & Wonder — Interactive Cards

*A little magic, one spin at a time.*

Welcome to **Whirl & Wonder**, a growing collection of interactive digital cards where scattered mosaic fragments gradually come together to reveal a surprise.

Combining creative coding, animation, and playful interaction, each card transforms a simple image into a little moment of discovery.

## KaleidoReveal — Interactive Mosaic Puzzle

**KaleidoReveal** is an interactive picture puzzle currently featuring three images, with more to come.

Unlike traditional puzzles, KaleidoReveal lets you bring an image together through circular motion. Move your mouse or finger around the screen to rotate the scattered fragments and gradually reveal the complete picture.

### Play Online

**[Play KaleidoReveal](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/cards/)**

No downloads, installations, or registration required. Simply open the link in your browser and start playing.

## How to Play

1. Open KaleidoReveal using the link above.
2. Choose an image to reveal.
3. Move your mouse or finger in circular motions to spin the scattered mosaic fragments.
4. Continue rotating until the fragments come together and the image is revealed.
5. Choose another image and enjoy the challenge again.

## About the Project

Whirl & Wonder explores how simple interactions can turn digital images into engaging, playful experiences.

The project brings together front-end development, visual effects, and interactive design, with the aim of creating digital cards that are enjoyable to explore and easy to share.
