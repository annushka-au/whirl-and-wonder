# Whirl & Wonder — Interactive Digital Art

*Three magical worlds. One simple gesture. Endless possibilities.*

Whirl & Wonder is a collection of three interactive digital art experiences inspired by movement, light, and nature.

## Start Here

**[Open Whirl & Wonder — Main Page](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/)**

The main page welcomes you with an animated introduction, delicate glowing moths, and three crystal dice. Hover over a die to see it glow, then click to explore its world. You can also skip the introduction.

## Direct Access to the Three Worlds

| Experience | Open page | What to explore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I — Interactive Cards** | [Play Interactive Cards](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/cards/) | Assemble five mosaic pictures by moving your mouse or finger in circles. Add a greeting and save your card. |
| **II — Two Directions, Two Secrets** | [Play Two Directions](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/two-directions/) | Rotate counterclockwise to reveal a snow leopard, clockwise to reveal a black panther. |
| **III — The Living Water** | [Play The Living Water](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/living-water/) | Create ripples and waves, awaken moths, summon golden fish, and watch the water lilies open. |

All three experiences run in a modern browser. No installation is required.

## Technology

HTML5 Canvas · CSS · JavaScript · Mouse and touch interactions
