# Whirl & Wonder: Interactive Art & Cards

*A little magic, one spin at a time.*

Welcome to **Whirl & Wonder**, a growing collection of interactive digital art and greeting cards where simple gestures reveal beautiful surprises.

Each experience combines creative coding, animation, and the magic of movement.

## Explore the Collection

### Whirl & Wonder I — Interactive Cards

[**Play Interactive Cards**](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/cards/)

Our original collection features five images:

- A sparkling rose
- An adorable puppy
- A blue-eyed husky
- A dramatic stormy sea
- A brown bear cub surrounded by nature

Move your mouse or finger in circles to assemble each image from scattered mosaic fragments. Add a personal greeting and save the finished card.

### Whirl & Wonder II — Two Directions, Two Secrets

[**Play Two Directions**](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/two-directions/)

Two different worlds are hidden inside one interactive mosaic:

- **Rotate counterclockwise (↶)** to reveal a snow leopard in the mountains.
- **Rotate clockwise (↷)** to reveal a black panther in a tropical jungle.
- **Reverse direction** to scatter one image and begin assembling the other.

*Two worlds. One turn.*

### Whirl & Wonder III — The Living Water

[**Play The Living Water**](https://annushka-au.github.io/whirl-and-wonder/living-water/)

Explore a moonlit lake that responds to your movements:

- **Move over the water** to create delicate ripples.
- **Move faster** to create stronger waves.
- **Move the pointer** to awaken tiny moths that flutter briefly and fade away.
- **Draw a circle on the water** to summon a golden fish with flowing fins.
- **Stop moving** and watch the water settle as the stars return to its surface.

Buttons provide an alternative way to summon fish and moths or calm the lake.

*Every movement awakens a little magic.*

## How to Play

1. Open an experience in a modern browser.
2. Use your mouse or finger over the interactive scene.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions; each chapter responds to different gestures.
4. Experiment with direction, speed, and circular movements.

Interactive Cards also supports personal greetings and image saving. Two Directions includes a PNG save option.

## Technology

- HTML5 Canvas
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Mouse and touch input
- Procedural animations and gesture detection

No frameworks or external libraries are required. The experiences work in modern desktop and mobile browsers.

## About the Project

Whirl & Wonder is an independent creative coding project exploring how movement, interaction, and visual storytelling can turn simple gestures into playful digital experiences.

Each chapter introduces a different interaction: revealing mosaics, reversing between worlds, or bringing a moonlit lake to life.
