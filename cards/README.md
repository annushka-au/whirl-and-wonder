# Whirl & Wonder: Interactive Cards

*A little magic, one spin at a time.*

Welcome to a collection of interactive digital greeting cards where scattered mosaic pieces gradually come together to reveal a surprise.

Each card combines creative coding, animation and a personal message.

## How to Play

1. Open an interactive card in your browser.
2. Move your mouse or finger in circles to spin the mosaic.
3. Watch the fragments gradually reveal an image.
4. Add your own greeting or personal wish.
5. Save your completed card as an image.

## The Collection

Our first creation features an adorable little puppy, with more surprises to come.

## Technology

Created with HTML5, CSS and JavaScript.

No installation or additional software required. Just open the card in a modern web browser.

## About

Whirl & Wonder is a personal creative coding experiment exploring how simple interactions can transform digital art into playful experiences.
